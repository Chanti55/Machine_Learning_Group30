Group 30 Version 19 predicts 90-day wholesale repeat purchases using a tuned GradientBoostingClassifier trained on 6,469 cleaned transactions across 44 curated features. Feature selection strictly eliminated identifier-like variables (customer_id and rare transport methods) and benchmarked all candidates against an empirical random_noise floor. Evaluated across 5 customer-grouped folds (GroupKFold), it achieves a mean ROC-AUC of 0.7903 and F1 of 0.7380, while maintaining a temporal generalization ROC-AUC of 0.7845 across 5 chronological splits. Predictions are calibrated at an operating decision threshold of tau = 0.50, projecting an expected repeat purchase rate of 64.7% across the 986 test purchase occasions. Context: Tuned Gradient Boosting (150 trees, lr=0.05, max_depth=2) on 44 Vote features at tau=0.50 (123 diffs vs v18).
