Group 30 Version 20 predicts 90-day wholesale repeat purchases using a tuned SoftBlendClassifier trained on 6,469 cleaned transactions across 44 curated features. Feature selection strictly eliminated identifier-like variables (customer_id and rare transport methods) and benchmarked all candidates against an empirical random_noise floor. Evaluated across 5 customer-grouped folds (GroupKFold), it achieves a mean ROC-AUC of 0.7964 and F1 of 0.7580, while maintaining a temporal generalization ROC-AUC of 0.7920 across 5 chronological splits. Predictions are calibrated at an operating decision threshold of tau = 0.45, projecting an expected repeat purchase rate of 71.9% across the 986 test purchase occasions. Context: Soft Blend Ensemble (45% GB + 35% LogReg + 20% RF) on 44 Vote features at tau=0.45 (65 diffs vs v18).
